About

Chaithanya Krishna Munagala Bengaluru, India SDE-2 at Alteryx IIT BHU, Varanasi Open to conversations

I'm currently working as a Backend SDE-2 at Alteryx. I'm passionate about distributed systems, data structures, low level design, high level design and competitive programming, and I work primarily with Java and Spring Boot. I like making an impact through code: shipping things that measurably move a number, not just close a ticket. I'm detail oriented and learning oriented, and I'd rather understand a system deeply than ship something I can't reason about.

I graduated from IIT (BHU), Varanasi in 2024 with a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering and a CGPA of 8.78. Campus life was a big part of those four years: I played a lot of sport and was active in E-Cell and the Cricket Club.

My first taste of shipping to real users was a Vue.JS internship at Blubirch in 2023. After graduating I joined DealShare as an SDE-1 on the Warehouse Management System team, where I spent two years building the Auto-Replenishment Engine, Kafka pipelines processing 30M+ events a day and APIs serving 2M+ hits a day, alongside on-call ownership of production workflows. I moved to Alteryx in 2026.

I also enjoy the product side of the work. Discovering what a requirement actually means, shaping the design around it, then building the thing myself from scratch. Taking a product from a blank page to something people genuinely use is the part of engineering I find most satisfying.
